FAQ

Good to know

Where is The Art Spa Tulum?

We are in La Veleta, Tulum, at Supermanzana 15, Manzana 69, 77760 Tulum, Quintana Roo, Mexico. La Veleta is a few minutes from downtown Tulum and on the way to the Hotel Zone.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day, Monday to Sunday, from 10:00 to 19:00 (Tulum time).

How do I book a massage or facial?

Book online 24/7 on our Book page and choose your treatment, date and time; you'll get instant confirmation. You can also message us on WhatsApp at +52 984 100 7119.

How much does a massage cost in Tulum at The Art Spa?

Massages start at $1,250 MXN for a 60-minute aromatherapy massage. Our Reset Ritual (massage + sauna + pool, 90 min) is $1,249 MXN, and couples massage packages start at $2,599 MXN for two people.

Do you offer couples massage?

Yes. We have a private room for couples and several couples rituals, from The Couple Ritual ($2,599 MXN for two, 90 min) to two-hour rituals with sauna, scrubs, wraps and massage.
