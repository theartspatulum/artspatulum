The Space

A spa that feels like a gallery

The Art Spa Tulum is the first spa in Tulum with a true gallery-inspired atmosphere, a space that encourages creativity, presence and pause.

Room 01

The Tea Area

Arrive a little early and settle in beneath a large hand-painted mural. Sip a cup of tea, let the art set the pace, and slow down before your treatment begins.

Room 02

The Sauna

A private hot sauna that opens the pores, warms the muscles and prepares the body for deeper work. Included in most rituals or bookable on its own.

Room 03

The Hydropool

A cold, bubbling pool for contrast therapy after the sauna. Hot, cold, repeat: the classic recipe for circulation and calm, on your own or together.

Room 04

The Massage Rooms

Calm, softly lit treatment rooms with linen curtains and jungle light, including a suite for couples rituals and side-by-side massage.
